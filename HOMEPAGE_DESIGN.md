# TIRAZÉ — Homepage Design Brief

## Creative direction
Moroccan Heritage Luxury. The homepage should feel like a refined Moroccan couture house: authentic craft, rich textile detail, editorial photography, calm ivory space, burgundy accents, and restrained antique-gold ornament.

## Audience and primary action
Women interested in traditional and contemporary Moroccan clothing, especially jellabas, and customers who want a garment made to their preferred design.

Primary conversion: start an inquiry on WhatsApp.
Secondary actions: explore the real-work gallery and follow Instagram / Facebook.

## Header
- Wordmark: TIRAZÉ.
- Small descriptor: COUTURE · BRODERIE · CRÉATION.
- Navigation: Accueil, Collections, Savoir-faire, À propos, Contact.
- Language control: Arabic / Français, once language scope is confirmed.
- A clear WhatsApp CTA.
- Mobile: compact header with accessible menu and persistent WhatsApp action.

## Hero
- Wide, editorial photograph of a real standout jellaba or couture piece supplied by the maker.
- Ivory or deep burgundy text panel with generous spacing.
- Eyebrow: MAISON DE COUTURE MAROCAINE.
- Headline: L'élégance de notre héritage.
- Supporting copy: Des créations marocaines pensées autour de votre style, entre tradition, couture et broderie.
- Primary CTA: Découvrir nos créations.
- Secondary CTA: Demander une création sur mesure.
- Do not use stock or generated imagery as a portfolio example. Until real hero photography is supplied, use a neutral branded placeholder rather than implying a garment is the maker's work.

## Section 1 — Featured creations
- Title: Nos créations.
- Lead with jellabas, the most requested category.
- Additional categories may include traditional and contemporary looks, embroidery, and children's clothing where genuine photos exist.
- Editorial card grid with consistent image ratios and minimal labels.
- Each item can link to WhatsApp with a prefilled inquiry referencing that item.

## Section 2 — Craft / Savoir-faire
- Split layout: genuine detail photograph on one side, concise text on the other.
- Explain sewing, pattern cutting, embroidery, and attention to finishing based on confirmed services.
- Avoid unsupported claims about specific techniques or materials.

## Section 3 — Made for you
- Heading: Une création à votre image.
- Explain that customers can choose a design and request a piece inspired by their idea.
- CTA: Parler de mon projet on WhatsApp.
- Ready-to-wear pieces can be mentioned only when available.

## Section 4 — About the maker
- Introduce the maker and her craft.
- Confirmed facts: diploma in sewing and pattern cutting; more than seven years of experience.
- Tone: personal, warm, professional; avoid invented biography or claims.

## Section 5 — Social gallery
- Heading: Les dernières créations.
- Invite visitors to follow Instagram and Facebook for new work and updates.
- Use real photos and actual profile links once provided.

## Visual system
- Burgundy #542C32
- Warm Ivory #F3EBDD
- Espresso #30231F
- Antique Gold #B99A67
- Display: Cormorant Garamond or comparable serif.
- Latin interface: Montserrat.
- Arabic: Noto Sans Arabic.
- Use fine gold rules, restrained Moroccan geometry, elegant spacing, and strong image hierarchy.

## UX and responsive behavior
- Mobile-first layout.
- Clear tap targets and readable type.
- Fast-loading optimized images.
- Accessible contrast and descriptive alt text.
- Right-to-left support for Arabic and left-to-right for French.
- WhatsApp CTA visible without overwhelming the design.

## Content still required before production

2. Real portfolio photos, ideally 15–30, including hero-quality jellaba photos.
3. Instagram and Facebook profile URLs.
4. Confirmed language set and default language.
5. Maker-approved biography and permission to publish the work photos.

## Explicit exclusions for first release
No customer accounts, database, online checkout, or admin dashboard. Keep inquiries on WhatsApp and publish a static site initially.
