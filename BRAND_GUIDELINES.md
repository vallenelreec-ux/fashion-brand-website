# TIRAZÉ — Brand Guidelines

## Brand direction
Moroccan Heritage Luxury: a refined Moroccan couture identity that celebrates traditional craftsmanship through a contemporary luxury presentation.

## Positioning
A Moroccan couture and embroidery atelier focused on jellabas, traditional and modern sewing, embroidery, and made-to-order designs for women and children.

## Brand promise
The elegance of Moroccan heritage, crafted with care and tailored to the person wearing it.

## Core messages
- Primary tagline: L'élégance de notre héritage.
- Supporting line: Couture marocaine, pensée pour vous.
- Descriptor: COUTURE · BRODERIE · CRÉATION

## Personality
Elegant, feminine, authentic, artistic, warm, meticulous, and confident. Avoid looking overly formal, mass-market, or excessively ornate.

## Color palette
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Burgundy | #542C32 | Primary brand color, key buttons, accents |
| Warm Ivory | #F3EBDD | Main backgrounds and negative space |
| Espresso | #30231F | Body text and deep contrast |
| Antique Gold | #B99A67 | Fine rules, icons, small ornamental accents |

Use ivory as the dominant surface, burgundy for identity and calls to action, espresso for readable text, and antique gold sparingly. Avoid large gold surfaces or gradients.

## Typography
- Display / wordmark direction: Cormorant Garamond (or a comparable elegant serif).
- Latin body and interface: Montserrat.
- Arabic: Noto Sans Arabic.
- Keep typography restrained; use no more than two or three font families.

## Logo direction
- Primary logo: TIRAZÉ wordmark in a refined serif, preserving the É accent.
- Secondary lockup: COUTURE · BRODERIE · CRÉATION beneath the wordmark.
- Monogram: a simple TÉ or TE mark for favicon, social avatar, labels, and packaging.
- Optional ornament: a very subtle line motif inspired by Moroccan embroidery. The wordmark must remain legible without it.
- Prepare burgundy-on-ivory, ivory-on-burgundy, and single-color versions.

## Graphic language
Use fine framing lines, generous whitespace, restrained geometry inspired by Moroccan craft, and subtle stitch-like details. Do not fill every section with zellige patterns or decorative motifs.

## Photography
- Portfolio images must be genuine photographs of the maker's own work.
- Prioritize full garment views, close-ups of embroidery and finishing, fabric details, and behind-the-scenes craftsmanship.
- Use soft natural or studio lighting, calm backgrounds, and consistent crops.
- AI-generated or stock imagery must never be presented as the maker's completed work. If used for mood references, keep it outside the portfolio.

## Social content pillars
1. Signature jellabas and featured pieces.
2. Embroidery and craftsmanship details.
3. Made-to-order process and customer-selected designs.
4. Behind the scenes: cutting, sewing, finishing.
5. Ready-to-wear pieces, only when actually available.

## Brand guardrails
- Do not claim luxury materials, handwork techniques, certifications, or services unless confirmed.
- Do not invent customer testimonials, prices, availability, or delivery promises.
- Do not use the existing shop name as the independent brand name.
- The name TIRAZÉ is a working candidate until domain, social handle, and trademark availability are checked.
